    🎙️ Reverse Voice Challenge

    Record a secret phrase, imitate your partner's reversed audio, and guess the double-reversed voice!

    1. Record 2. Imitate 3. Guess 4. Reveal
    Phase 1 of 4 · Original Record

    Record Your Secret Word or Short Phrase

    Type your secret word (so we can reveal it in Phase 4) and record a 1–5 second voice clip. Once both players finish, your recordings are swapped!

    Click "Start Recording" and speak clearly into your microphone.
    Phase 2 of 4 · Imitate Reversed Audio

    Listen to Partner's Reversed Audio & Imitate It!

    We reversed your partner's audio using Web Audio PCM arrays. Listen closely to the alien-like reversed clip, then record your own vocal imitation of those sounds!

    Listen to the reversed audio first, then mimic the sounds!
    Phase 3 of 4 · Guess Partner's Secret Word

    Listen to Your Locally Reversed Imitation & Guess!

    We locally reversed your OWN vocal imitation using AudioContext! Listen to how your reversed imitation sounds and guess your partner's original word:

    Listen to your locally reversed imitation, then submit your guess!
